# PrepDeck — Product Roadmap (2026)

The roadmap is reviewed every quarter. Last review: 1 April 2026.

## Q1 2026 — Shipped

- Personalised problem recommendations based on user's last 20 attempts
- Mobile app launched on Android (India only)
- AI mentor switched from GPT-4o to Claude Haiku 4.5 for code review
- New onboarding flow — time-to-first-problem dropped from 8 minutes to 2 minutes

## Q2 2026 — In Progress

- iOS app launch (target: 15 June 2026)
- Behavioural-interview track (50 new mock interview scenarios)
- Group study rooms — 4-person video study sessions with shared whiteboard
- Hindi-language UI (system text only — problem statements remain in English)

## Q3 2026 — Planned

- Mobile app launch in the US
- Live cohort feature — instructors run synchronous 4-week cohorts inside the app
- Spaced repetition for forgotten concepts — daily reinforcement queue
- Direct integration with LeetCode and HackerRank for cross-platform progress

## Q4 2026 — Exploratory

- Enterprise tier for university career centres
- Hardware interview track (system design + low-level)
- Open-source contributor onboarding track

## Out of Scope for 2026

- Bootcamps with live instructors (we are a self-serve platform)
- Job-board feature (partners with Sourcegraph and AngelList instead)
- Salary negotiation coaching (deferred to 2027)
