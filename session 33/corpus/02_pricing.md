# PrepDeck — Pricing and Refund Policy

PrepDeck offers three plans. All prices are listed in USD; Indian customers are
billed in INR at a regional purchasing-parity rate (roughly 35% of the USD price).

## Plans

**Starter — Free**
- Access to the public problem set (300+ problems)
- Community discussion board
- No AI mentor access
- No mock interviews

**Pro — $29 per month** (India: ₹849 per month)
- Everything in Starter
- AI mentor: unlimited code review and feedback
- 10 mock interviews per month
- Resume review (1 per month)
- Cancel anytime

**Pro Annual — $290 per year** (India: ₹8,490 per year)
- Everything in Pro, billed annually
- 2 months free vs monthly
- Priority support

## Refund Policy

Pro and Pro Annual customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of the
original purchase, no questions asked. After 14 days, refunds are evaluated on a
case-by-case basis and require a written reason. Refund requests are processed
within 5 business days.

Annual customers who cancel after the 14-day window receive a prorated refund
for unused months, less a 10% administrative fee.

The Free plan has no refund (nothing was charged).
