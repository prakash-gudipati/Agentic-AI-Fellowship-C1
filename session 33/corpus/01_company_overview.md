# PrepDeck — Company Overview

PrepDeck is a career-preparation platform for engineering students and early-career
software developers. The company was founded in March 2024 and is headquartered in
Bengaluru, India. A second office was opened in Austin, Texas in November 2025.

The mission of PrepDeck is simple: help working students in India and the US ship
interview-ready portfolios in 12 weeks or less. The product combines structured
practice with a personal AI mentor that reviews code, suggests fixes, and runs
mock interviews on demand.

PrepDeck serves roughly 18,000 active students across India and the US as of
April 2026. About 62% of users are in India and 31% are in the US, with the
remaining 7% spread across the UK, Singapore, and the UAE.

The company is privately funded and has not announced a Series A. The seed round
of $2.4 million closed in October 2024.

Tagline: *Ship your portfolio, not just your resume.*
