# PrepDeck — Hiring FAQ

This document answers the most common candidate questions about PrepDeck's
engineering hiring process.

## Levels

PrepDeck has four engineering levels:
- **L2 — Engineer** (0–2 years experience)
- **L3 — Senior Engineer** (2–5 years)
- **L4 — Staff Engineer** (5+ years and strong scope)
- **L5 — Principal Engineer** (currently held only by the CTO)

## Interview Loop

The full loop is four rounds plus a recruiter screen:
1. **Recruiter screen** — 30 minutes, scheduling and basic fit
2. **Technical screen** — 60 minutes, one coding question (data structures)
3. **System design** — 60 minutes, real-world design (L3+ only; L2 candidates
   get a debugging round here instead)
4. **AI engineering deep-dive** — 75 minutes, candidate walks through one
   project they have shipped that involved LLMs or agents
5. **Values round** — 45 minutes, behavioural and team-fit

Total loop is roughly 4 hours of candidate time, spread across 1–2 weeks.

## What We Look For

The team weights three signals: ship rate (have you actually finished and
shipped projects?), debugging instinct (do you read tracebacks calmly?), and
written communication (can you summarise a problem in 4 sentences?).

PrepDeck is not a leetcode-grinder shop. The technical screen uses real-world
problems, not memorised tricks. Candidates are encouraged to think out loud.

## Locations and Offices

PrepDeck currently hires in two locations:
- **Bengaluru, India** (Indiranagar office, hybrid: 3 days in office)
- **Austin, Texas** (downtown WeWork, hybrid: 2 days in office)

The team does not currently sponsor work visas for India-to-US relocations.
Remote roles are reviewed case-by-case for L4 and L5 only.

## Compensation Philosophy

PrepDeck pays at the top of the local market band for each level. The team
does not publish band ranges externally. Equity is offered at L3 and above.

For India candidates, salaries are denominated in INR. For US candidates,
salaries are denominated in USD. No conversion or geo-arbitrage games.

## Applying

Send a one-paragraph note and a link to one shipped project to
hiring@prepdeck.io. Cover letters are optional. Resumes are optional if you
include a link to a portfolio site or GitHub profile.
